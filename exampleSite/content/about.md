This site is built with **SciDraft**, a modern Hugo theme for scientific
writing and research — from quick research notes to comprehensive articles.

## What SciDraft provides

- **One content type, any length.** Everything is a note. Short items render
  in full inside the feed; long articles use an explicit
  `{{</* preview */>}}…{{</* /preview */>}}` marker pair to choose what the
  card shows, with a "Read more" link to the full text.
- **A notes feed homepage.** A fast, feed-style list of notes with identity
  labels (institute / author), timestamps, tags, and rotating promotions.
- **Scientific writing support.** BibTeX citations from `assets/bib/refs.bib`
  (`{{</* references */>}}`), math typeset by KaTeX at build time, mermaid
  diagrams, pseudo-algorithm blocks, and galleries.
- **Occupation reference pages.** Auto-generated category and alphabetical
  indexes over notes that carry occupation metadata.
- **Built-in search.** A client-side search page indexes every note and
  returns results as you type, with math-aware preview rendering.
- **Light and dark themes.** A one-click toggle switches the whole site,
  remembering the visitor's preference.
- **RSS and sensible defaults.** Feeds for the site and each section, and a
  minimal config that works out of the box — a new site needs only a base
  URL, a title, and the theme name.
