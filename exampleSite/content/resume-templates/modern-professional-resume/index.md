This bundle holds the resume-template images used by the visual promotion
({{</* promotion-visual */>}}), which resolves them through this page's
resources. It is **headless**: Hugo does not render a page for it, but the
images stay published and available to the shortcode.

The example page that used to live here — showing the retired
{{</* gallery */>}} / {{</* gallery-item */>}} shortcodes over these same
images — is preserved at
`retired-functions/function-archive/gallery/example-page-with-gallery.md`.
